Dashboard Intelligence Notes
🔴 PSU asset-quality compression is the cleanest trend in the panel — Bank of India GNPA 4.41% → 1.81% and Canara 3.73% → 1.57% over eight quarters. Indian Bank now runs a 0.15% net NPA.
⚖️ IndusInd is the outlier. Losses of ₹2,236cr (Q4FY25) and ₹445cr (Q2FY26) followed the derivatives accounting episode; GNPA peaked at 3.64% and has since eased to 3.25%, with PAT back to ₹1,002cr.
💰 NBFC gross NPAs sit structurally higher — Shriram 4.64%, Chola 4.50% on RBI norms — because the underlying asset mix is used-vehicle and MSME credit, not because of distress. Read them against their own history.
📐 Two Q1FY27 results need adjusting before comparison: Bank of Baroda's ₹1,278cr PAT is after a ₹5,680cr NMC Health settlement (₹5,528cr ex-item), and IDFC First's ₹1,075cr is a record, up 132% YoY.
Methodology & Sources

What's here: reported revenue, profit after tax, gross NPA ratio and net NPA ratio for eight quarters, Q2FY25 (Sep 2024) through Q1FY27 (Jun 2026). Revenue YoY is derived from a twelve-quarter revenue series (four quarters deeper than the display window) so it resolves for every displayed quarter, and is suppressed where the year-ago base is missing or non-meaningful. Market cap and RoA are carried in the heatmap for ranking convenience but are single point-in-time values at 30 Jul 2026, marked with an asterisk — they do not move with the quarter slider. Figures are standalone unless a company only publishes consolidated numbers — Jio Financial and L&T Finance are consolidated, and IndusInd's Q1FY27 consolidated PAT was ₹1,037cr against the ₹1,002cr standalone shown here.

Sources: company-reported quarterly results as aggregated by screener.in, cross-checked against company press releases and results coverage for Q1FY27. Where a company had not reported at the time of this update — SBI (board meeting 7 Aug 2026), Bajaj Finance (30 Jul 2026) and Aditya Birla Capital (31 Jul 2026) — the cell is left blank rather than estimated.

Known limits: Yes Bank's NPA ratios are carried at one decimal place at source, so small moves are not resolvable. Chola and L&T Finance report Gross Stage 3 under Ind AS; Chola's RBI-norms GNPA is used here for comparability, and L&T Finance's Gross Stage 3 history is incomplete. Jio Financial and Aditya Birla Capital are grouped separately because bank asset-quality and margin metrics do not describe them meaningfully — Jio Financial's lending AUM was ₹30,667cr against a consolidated PAT driven largely by asset management and treasury. Three later additions carry their own caveats: IDBI Bank is classified by the RBI as a private-sector bank since 2019 but is grouped with the PSUs here pending divestment; Tata Capital is on a consolidated basis, began disclosing quarterly NPA only from Q2FY26, and its FY25 revenue step-up reflects the Tata Motors Finance merger rather than organic growth; HDB Financial listed in 2025, so its NPA ratios for the most recent one to two quarters are not yet carried at source.

Snapshot tab: market capitalisation, P/E, P/BV, RoE and RoA are a market snapshot at 30 Jul 2026, sourced from StockScans peer-comparison exports (Bank of India and Shriram Finance from screener.in, which StockScans does not cover in these peer sets). RoE and RoA there are trailing figures, not quarterly — they are deliberately kept in a separate column group from the Q1FY27 reported figures so the two are not read as the same thing.

Still not shown: NIM and CASA have no quarterly time series here. NIM is disclosed on inconsistent bases (domestic vs global, total assets vs interest-earning assets, NIM+fees for some NBFCs) and CASA is rarely given as a ratio in quarterly releases, so both appear only as a Q1FY27 snapshot where the company stated them. CRAR is not tracked. Building real quarterly series for these needs bank-by-bank investor presentations rather than an aggregator.
