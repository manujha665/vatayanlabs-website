The Bigger Picture for Indian Investors
India's own hyperscaler build-out (Google, Microsoft, Amazon data centers in Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad) will eventually require this same optical infrastructure. The supply chain that Lumentum is building in the US is the same supply chain that will power Indian data centers. Indirectly, Lumentum's technology will be inside the AI infrastructure serving India — even if Lumentum has no direct India revenue line today.
