When Currency Hurts
If INR appreciates vs USD (rare, but possible if RBI intervenes aggressively or FII inflows surge), your USD gains shrink in INR terms. A 5% rupee appreciation on a 17% USD gain leaves you with only ~12% in INR. The currency works both ways.
