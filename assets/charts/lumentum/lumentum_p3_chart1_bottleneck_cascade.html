Why This Pattern Matters for Investors
Each bottleneck creates a multi-year growth window for the companies that can relieve it. NVIDIA captured the GPU bottleneck. HBM benefited SK Hynix and Micron. The optical bottleneck of 2026 belongs to Lumentum and Coherent — the only companies with the fabrication capability to ship 200G EML chips at the volume hyperscalers need. McKinsey projects this shortfall persists through 2027 at minimum.
Source: McKinsey analysis (June 2025), mlq.ai Optical Networking Research (March 2026), Lumentum Holdings earnings calls. HBM = High Bandwidth Memory. EML = Electro-Absorption Modulated Laser.
