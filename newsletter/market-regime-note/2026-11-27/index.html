The short version

The index rested. The buyers did not.

The Nifty fell 0.7% for the week, its first weekly loss since the October washout, and Friday’s 1.1% drop came from banks and metals. Underneath, the flows held: FIIs bought on every session, and domestic institutions were net buyers for the week for the first time in November. Breadth is still the missing piece.

Where is the money flowing?

Still in through the front door. FIIs bought ₹17,630 cr in four sessions, net buyers every day, taking November to ₹1,29,579 cr. DIIs net bought ₹6,333 cr after three weeks of selling.

Which index is doing well?

Banks, and now pharma. Nifty Private Bank tops StockScans index strength at 93.3. Nifty Pharma scores 90.0 and sits 0.4% from its one-year high. Nifty IT climbed to 76.8 after a 4.6% week.

How is kospi doing?

It fell 6.2% on Wednesday to 4,697.51, its lowest close since mid-January, then jumped 4.3% on Friday to 4,915.68 on foreign buying. Up 0.9% on the week, still 46.1% below its June peak.

What is happening to soxx?

Whipsawed. Up about 14% on Monday on Nvidia leadership news, down 8.3% on Tuesday, $127.98 at Wednesday’s close. Still about 32% below end-September.

Signs of regime change?

In leadership, yes. In participation, still no. 45.4% of stocks sit above their 200-day EMA, and 148 of 247 industries are underperforming, up from 127 a week ago.

Where are we in the relay?

Still leg one, with a first stir from the feeder. The one-month return still falls down the cap ladder, from +16.8% for Nifty Bank to +2.5% for the Microcap 250 (Exhibit 1), but DIIs bought this week.

The scorecard: Leadership has changed regime. The market has not yet. Four signals in place, four mixed, two missing.
